# References

The implementation uses standard mathematical definitions from these sources. The
references inform formulas and terminology; this repository does not copy their
source code.

1. Rob J. Hyndman and George Athanasopoulos, *Forecasting: Principles and Practice*,
   3rd edition, OTexts, 2021: https://otexts.com/fpp3/
2. Charles C. Holt, "Forecasting Seasonals and Trends by Exponentially Weighted
   Moving Averages", ONR Memorandum 52, 1957.
3. Peter R. Winters, "Forecasting Sales by Exponentially Weighted Moving Averages",
   *Management Science*, 6(3), 1960.
4. G. E. P. Box, G. M. Jenkins, G. C. Reinsel, and G. M. Ljung, *Time Series
   Analysis: Forecasting and Control*, 5th edition, Wiley, 2015.
5. R. J. Hyndman and A. B. Koehler, "Another Look at Measures of Forecast Accuracy",
   *International Journal of Forecasting*, 22(4), 2006.

`cn-wn/moonsignalkit`: https://mooncakes.io/docs/cn-wn/moonsignalkit
